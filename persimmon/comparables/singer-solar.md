# Singer Solar — Beauregard Parish Comparative Project File

**Classification:** PRIMARY/SECONDARY SOURCE RESEARCH FILE  
**Purpose:** Comparison project for the Persimmon/nuclear investigation. Inclusion does not imply wrongdoing by the developer, landowners, public officials, or utilities.

## Project identity

- **Project:** Singer Solar
- **Developer/entity:** Singer Solar, LLC, a NextEra Energy Resources subsidiary
- **Location:** Beauregard Parish, near Singer/DeRidder
- **Solar capacity:** 200 MW in NextEra's current project materials and the 2024 U.S. Army Corps public notice
- **Developer-described footprint:** approximately 1,970 acres
- **Federal permit tract:** 2,661 acres in the Army Corps public notice; permit drawings identify a project area of approximately 2,706.78 acres
- **Developer-estimated capital investment:** $350 million
- **Developer-estimated local property-tax revenue:** approximately $38 million over the first 35 years
- **Developer-estimated construction employment:** up to 250 jobs

The different acreage figures appear to describe different concepts (developer project footprint versus federal permit tract/project area) and should not be treated as contradictory without reviewing the underlying site plans.

Documented impacts include:
- 11.42 acres of degraded pine-plantation wetlands permanently impacted through fill/access roads/structures;
- 3.14 acres of bottomland hardwoods permanently impacted;
- 976.17 acres of wetland pine-plantation habitat permanently converted to herbaceous habitat through mechanized clearing and installation of panels/appurtenances;
- 4.56 acres of bottomland hardwoods similarly converted;
- approximately 59,100 cubic yards of earthen material proposed for deposition into wetlands for roads, switchyard and substation.

## Investigative relevance

Singer is an important comparison to Persimmon because it is another industrial-scale solar development in the same broader Southwest Louisiana political region, but in Beauregard Parish rather than Moss Bluff.

Questions for comparison:
- Which state legislators publicly supported, opposed, or remained silent while Singer advanced?
- What did Charles Owen, Rodney Schamerhorn, Brett Geymann and other regional legislators say during the 2023–2025 approval/permitting period?
- What local opposition existed, if any?
- How were the PILOT terms calculated and distributed among taxing bodies?
- What setbacks, drainage protections, decommissioning requirements and land-use conditions apply?
- How many residences lie within 1/4, 1/2 and 1 mile?
- How does the project's wetland/forest conversion compare with Persimmon?
- What was the precise SLEMCO procurement chronology?
- Did any elected officials involved in later anti-solar advocacy participate in Singer approvals, meetings, tours or economic-development discussions?

Absence of a public statement is not evidence of support or opposition.
