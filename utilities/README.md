# Utility / Offtake Research

This section examines the wholesale market structure behind utility-scale generation relevant to the Persimmon investigation.

A solar developer obtaining land and permits does not by itself establish a commercially viable operating project. Orion Renewables publicly states that a project requires a reliable and creditworthy power purchaser or a liquid energy market, and that power sales may be made to utilities, commercial/industrial customers, or power marketers. Orion also describes asset-purchase and build-transfer structures in which utilities purchase development assets or completed projects.

Accordingly, this investigation distinguishes:

1. **Developer / generator** — develops, finances, owns or sells the generating asset.
2. **Offtaker / wholesale purchaser** — purchases electricity/capacity through a PPA or similar contract.
3. **Utility owner** — may purchase the entire completed project through a build-own-transfer or asset-purchase transaction.
4. **Transmission / balancing / market operator** — provides grid access or market functions but is not necessarily the purchaser.
5. **Retail utility/cooperative** — sells electricity to homes/businesses/end users.
6. **End-use customer** — ultimately consumes electricity and pays retail or negotiated rates.

The citizenry are generally downstream retail consumers in this structure, but utilities are not necessarily the only possible first contractual purchasers. Large commercial/industrial customers and power marketers can also serve as offtakers, and merchant projects may transact into organized wholesale markets.

## Core reporting question

For every project under review, identify:

- Who is expected to buy the electricity?
- Who buys capacity and ancillary services, if applicable?
- Is there a signed PPA?
- Is the project merchant, contracted, or intended for sale to a utility?
- Who owns the interconnection rights?
- Which transmission owner / balancing authority receives the project?
- Who ultimately bears procurement costs?
- What LPSC approval is required?
- What incentives, tax structures, or cost-recovery mechanisms depend on the utility transaction?

Do not equate interconnection with power purchase.
