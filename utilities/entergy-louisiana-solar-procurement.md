# Entergy Louisiana — Solar Procurement / Offtake File

**Classification:** PRIMARY-SOURCE RESEARCH FILE

## Why Entergy matters

Entergy Louisiana is not merely a passive transmission pathway for renewable development. It has repeatedly and deliberately procured utility-scale solar through long-term power-purchase agreements and utility-ownership structures.

## Procurement chronology

### 2021 ELL Solar RFP
Source: https://rfp.entergy.com/ENTRFP/SEND/2021ELLSolarRFP/Index.htm

Entergy Louisiana solicited up to **600 MW** of solar resources. Final results announced January 27, 2022 said ELL selected several resources expected to provide at least 600 MW through a balance of:
- Power Purchase Agreements (PPAs)
- Build-Own-Transfer (BOT) proposals

### 2021 Solar Portfolio / 475 MW
Sources:
https://www.entergy.com/news/entergy-louisiana-purchase-475-megawatts-solar-power
https://www.entergy.com/news/entergy-louisiana-receives-approval-purchase-475-mw-solar-power-add-green-tariff-option

Entergy sought/received approval for four Louisiana solar resources totaling 475 MW:
- Vacherie — 150 MW
- Sunlight Road — 50 MW
- St. Jacques — 150 MW
- Elizabeth — 125 MW

Entergy said it would purchase output from Vacherie, Sunlight Road and Elizabeth under **20-year PPAs**. St. Jacques was structured as a BOT, meaning Entergy would own and maintain the facility after construction.

### 2022 ELL Renewables RFP
Source: https://rfp.entergy.com/ENTRFP/SEND/2022ELLRenewablesRFP/

ELL solicited **1,500 MW** of renewable resources. Final results announced March 17, 2023 included both PPAs and BOT proposals.

### 3 GW Solar / Solar+Storage procurement
LPSC notice:
https://lpsc.louisiana.gov/docs/news/Notice-of-ELL-3-GW-RFP-and-Bidder-Conference.pdf

RFP:
https://rfp.entergy.com/ENTRFP/SEND/ELL3GWSOLARRFP/Index.htm

Documents:
https://rfp.entergy.com/ENTRFP/SEND/ELL3GWSolarRFP/Documents.htm

Under LPSC Order U-36697, Entergy Louisiana received an alternative/streamlined procurement mechanism for up to **3,000 MW of solar and/or solar-plus-storage resources**.

The procurement documents expressly include:
- model solar BOT agreement;
- model solar PPA;
- developmental-solar due diligence;
- credit/collateral requirements;
- accounting certification;
- proposal pricing/energy quantities.

Procurement Window 4 concluded June 1, 2026, with selected resources to be disclosed after definitive agreements.

## Existing PPA example

Sunlight Road Solar entered commercial operation in November 2024 and supplies Entergy Louisiana through a **20-year PPA**.

## Investigative significance

The economics of Louisiana solar development cannot be understood solely through developers, parish land-use approvals, or landowners. Entergy Louisiana has operated as a major wholesale buyer and prospective owner of solar generation.

This creates several reporting questions:

- Did Persimmon participate in any Entergy RFP or procurement window?
- Did Orion or an affiliate register for Entergy's 3 GW solicitation?
- Is Persimmon intended for an Entergy PPA, BOT/asset sale, direct C&I offtake, power marketer, or merchant market sale?
- Did Southern Prairie/Westbridge participate?
- Which Entergy procurement windows correspond chronologically with development of Persimmon and Southern Prairie?
- What resources were selected but remain undisclosed pending definitive agreements?
- How does Entergy's aggressive solar procurement coexist with the state's simultaneous nuclear-development strategy?
- Are large new industrial/data-center loads changing Entergy's preferred generation mix?

No evidence currently establishes Entergy as the purchaser of Persimmon or Southern Prairie. That must be documented before asserting it.
