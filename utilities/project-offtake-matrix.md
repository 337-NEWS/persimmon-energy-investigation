## Singer

An LPSC-hosted resource-planning document states that SLEMCO selected a **25-year solar PPA** with the approximately 200 MW Singer Solar project with NextEra Energy Resources.

Source:
https://lpscpubvalence.lpsc.louisiana.gov/portal/PSC/ViewFile?fileId=iPlUWMNRZO4%3D

This establishes an identified wholesale buyer for Singer.

## Southern Prairie

Westbridge says Southern Prairie has selected a point of interconnection:
https://www.westbridge.energy/southern-prairie/

Local reporting says project transmission would feed into the Jeff Davis Electric Cooperative grid. **That does not establish JDEC as the purchaser.** Interconnection and offtake are separate contractual/economic relationships.

Current priority: identify any PPA, tolling agreement, C&I offtake, merchant-market plan, RFP selection or asset-sale arrangement.

## Persimmon

Orion's Persimmon site currently markets the project's size and economics but the sources reviewed to date have not established a purchaser:
https://persimmonenergyproject.com/

Orion's general development model says a reliable creditworthy purchaser or liquid market must be identified and describes power sales to utilities, C&I customers or power marketers, as well as project/asset sales to utilities:
https://orionrenewables.com/how-we-work/

Current priority: determine whether Persimmon has:
- participated in an Entergy Louisiana procurement;
- secured or negotiated an offtake agreement;
- targeted a large industrial/data-center customer;
- targeted a power marketer;
- planned merchant MISO sales;
- contemplated sale of the project to a utility.

Do not identify Entergy, BECi, 1803 or JDEC as Persimmon's buyer without documentary evidence.
