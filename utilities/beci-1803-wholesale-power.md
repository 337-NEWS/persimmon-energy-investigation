# BECi / 1803 — Wholesale Power Structure

**Classification:** PRIMARY-SOURCE RESEARCH FILE

## Structure

Beauregard Electric Cooperative (BECi) is a distribution cooperative and member of **1803 Electric Cooperative**.

BECi explanation:
https://www.beci.org/faqs/what-is-1803/

1803:
https://www.1803electric.coop/about-us/who-we-are/

1803 was organized in 2019 by Louisiana distribution cooperatives to procure wholesale power. Its initial portfolio of PPAs was approved by the Louisiana Public Service Commission in January 2022. 1803 began serving its first member January 1, 2025 and remaining members April 1, 2025. Its member cooperatives collectively serve approximately 500,000 end users.

1803 also performs transmission-related operations, has integrated its transmission system into MISO, operates as a Local Balancing Authority and has stated plans to become a MISO Transmission Owner.

## Solar economics — 1803's own presentation

August 16, 2023 LPSC presentation:
https://lpsc.louisiana.gov/docs/news/Presentation-to-LPSC-Aug-2023-For-Distribution.pdf

1803 told the LPSC:
- its Bayou Galion 98 MW solar PPA had been terminated;
- Bayou Chicot and Bayou Teche remained in its portfolio;
- MISO capacity-rule changes meant those solar PPAs did not provide winter capacity;
- solar still had energy value because it was not exposed to fuel-price volatility;
- the value depended on the actual PPA price per MWh;
- it compared solar PPA pricing directly against equivalent natural-gas fuel costs.

The presentation also explains contractual termination protections for both 1803 ratepayers and project developers if regulatory approval, project costs or financing changed materially.

## Investigative significance

For BECi customers, BECi is not independently shopping project-by-project for all generation in the conventional sense. 1803 functions as the wholesale procurement layer for its member cooperatives.

That means the relevant documentary chain is often:

**generator/developer → PPA/resource transaction → 1803 → BECi → BECi retail member**

This makes 1803 procurement records at least as important as BECi records for determining what generation BECi customers ultimately finance and consume.
