# 1803 Electric Cooperative — Research Report

Research conducted 2026-10-05 (UTC). Mission: investigate 1803 Electric Cooperative (Louisiana G&T), its owners/board, Tommy Cryar's role, the solar portfolio, Kevin Turner's statements, and a "nuclear is definitely the future" quote attributed to Cryar — plus follow-up leads involving the ALEC directory, timeline correction, June 2026 BECi minutes, and LPSC Docket X-37183 / Attachment 8(a)-(b).

## Summary

- **Owners / formation:** 1803 was organized in 2019 by five Louisiana distribution cooperatives: Beauregard Electric Cooperative, Claiborne Electric Cooperative, Northeast Louisiana Power Cooperative, South Louisiana Electric Cooperative Association, and Washington-St. Tammany Electric Cooperative. It was LPSC-certified in 2021; its initial power-supply portfolio was approved in January 2022. Service began for NELPCO Jan. 1, 2025 and the other original members in April 2025.
- **Board:** The current public board page lists 14 directors, two each from seven member cooperatives. BECi's two representatives are **Michael Viator** and **Ricky Deggs**.
- **Tommy Cryar:** Cryar is **not** on the current 1803 board. He is president of the BECi board, District 9 director, and also sits on the ALEC board.
- **Solar portfolio:** The LPSC-approved portfolio included Bayou Galion (98 MWac), Bayou Chicot (95 MW), and Bayou Teche (150 MW). The Bayou Galion PPA was later terminated.
- **Kevin Turner:** NRECA/cooperative.com quoted then-BECi interim GM Kevin Turner explaining that the five cooperatives combined demand to attract large-scale power suppliers. The described portfolio included gas generation, a Constellation/MISO arrangement, and solar agreements.
- **Capacity RFP:** June 2, 2026 BECi minutes state Viator and Deggs presented an 1803 update concerning an RFP process to satisfy minimum capacity-compliance obligations, including evaluation of portfolio options.
- **Nuclear quote:** The exact phrase “nuclear is definitely the future” attributed to Cryar was not found in indexed public sources during this research. Separately, reporter-held screenshots document the statement and should be preserved with context as primary evidence.
- **LPSC X-37183:** The BECi investigative-audit docket is confirmed. A referenced “PUBLIC Attachment 8(a)-(b)” concerning board benefits/compensation was not located in the public material reviewed and remains a records-request target.

## 1803 ownership and timeline

The five original owner-members were:

1. Beauregard Electric Cooperative, Inc. (DeRidder)
2. Claiborne Electric Cooperative, Inc. (Homer)
3. Northeast Louisiana Power Cooperative, Inc. (Winnsboro)
4. South Louisiana Electric Cooperative Association (Houma)
5. Washington-St. Tammany Electric Cooperative, Inc. (Franklinton)

Pointe Coupee EMC later joined as a transmission-only member. Current public governance material also shows Concordia Electric and PC Electric represented, bringing the public board roster to 14.

Key dates:
- **2019:** 1803 organized/established.
- **July 2, 2021:** LPSC Order S-35709 certified 1803 as a wholesale G&T.
- **Jan. 28, 2022:** LPSC Order U-35927 approved the initial power-supply portfolio.
- **Jan. 1, 2025:** 1803 began serving NELPCO.
- **April 2025:** the other original distribution members began taking service after prior contracts expired.

## Board and BECi representation

Current public board material identifies **Michael Viator** and **Ricky Deggs** as BECi's two 1803 directors. BECi's Nov. 4, 2025 agenda and June 2, 2026 minutes corroborate that Viator and Deggs deliver the 1803 board/update reports.

LPSC testimony from June 2024 stated that 1803 governance consists of two representatives from each member cooperative.

### Tommy Cryar

No public record reviewed placed Tommy Cryar on the 1803 board. His documented role is president of the Beauregard Electric Cooperative board, representing District 9. Public material also identifies him as an ALEC board member.

The June 2, 2026 BECi minutes state the meeting was called to order by “Mr. Tommy Cryar, President.”

## Solar portfolio

The LPSC-approved portfolio included **343 MWac** across three solar PPAs:

1. **Bayou Galion** — Morehouse Parish near Mer Rouge; 98 MWac; Recurrent Energy/Canadian Solar. The PPA was later terminated. An Aug. 16, 2023 LPSC presentation states the 98 MW PPA had been terminated pursuant to contract terms; BECi's May 2023 minutes described the northeast Louisiana solar project as canceled because of pricing issues.
2. **Bayou Chicot** — Evangeline Parish; 95 MW in the 1803 PPA material.
3. **Bayou Teche** — St. Mary Parish; 150 MW in the 1803 PPA material.

A Sept. 29, 2026 Reuters report described Exus Renewables acquiring development rights to Bayou Teche and Bayou Chicot using different project nameplate figures. The current status of the original 1803 PPAs was not established in this research.

## Kevin Turner and 1803 procurement

NRECA's cooperative.com article, “G&T Focus: Five Louisiana Co-ops Form a New G&T,” identified Kevin Turner as BECi's interim general manager and quoted him explaining the procurement rationale:

> “With the changing face of modern power procurement come challenges to the way electric cooperatives secure the power their member-owners need.”

The article also quoted Turner saying that by uniting, the cooperatives created a larger, more attractive power demand for large-scale power brokers.

The portfolio described in the same article included:
- 58% of the capacity/energy/ancillary services of a Kindle Energy combined-cycle plant under a 20-year commitment;
- capacity/energy from an existing Calpine combined-cycle plant;
- a five-year Constellation Energy arrangement involving MISO; and
- agreements with several solar facilities.

No KPLC story quoting Turner about 1803 was located during the research.

## June 2026 capacity RFP

BECi's June 2, 2026 minutes state:

> “Mr. Viator and Mr. Deggs presented the 1803 update regarding the RFP process to satisfy minimum compliance obligations related to capacity, including evaluation of portfolio options.”

The same minutes report the transition to Ron Repsher as 1803 CEO effective June 1, 2026, succeeding Brian Hobbs. The spelling of Repsher's name differs in the BECi minutes and other records and should be checked against the original before quotation.

The underlying RFP, eligible technologies, bidder/proposer information, evaluation criteria, and final selections remain investigative targets.

That search result should not be confused with the reporter's separately preserved screenshots, which show Cryar making the statement in a public Facebook discussion. For publication, preserve the screenshot, visible context, retrieval date, and original URL if available.

## LPSC Docket X-37183

Docket X-37183 is an LPSC investigative audit into BECi and its board concerning potential violations of policies, practices, or procedures that could cause cost or service impacts.

Public material located included:
- LPSC's data request to BECi;
- BECi's June 27, 2024 public-version response; and
- KPLC-uploaded records concerning the investigation.

A document referenced as **“PUBLIC Attachment 8(a)-(b)”** concerning board benefits/compensation for 2021–2024 was not located among the material reviewed. It remains a records-request target.

## Nuclear-policy timeline lead

May 22, 2024 LPSC minutes state that a June Business & Executive session would be held in Metairie to coincide with the **Louisiana Advanced Nuclear Competitive Edge** project/event scheduled for June 17–18 at Copeland Towers.

This establishes a dated Louisiana advanced-nuclear policy discussion in 2024. It does **not** by itself establish participation by Cryar, BECi, 1803, Geymann, or any specific developer. Attendee lists, invitations, presentations, correspondence, and expenses have been requested.
