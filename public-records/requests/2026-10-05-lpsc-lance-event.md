# Public Records Request — Louisiana Advanced Nuclear Competitive Edge — June 17–18, 2024

**Sent:** 2026-10-05  
**To:** lpscpublicrecords@la.gov  
**From:** Dalton Barron, Publisher / Investigative Journalist, 337.NEWS

## Scope

Records from January 1 through July 31, 2024 concerning the **Louisiana Advanced Nuclear Competitive Edge** event/project held June 17–18, 2024 in Metairie, including:

- agendas, programs, presentations, briefing material, handouts, and reports;
- invitations, attendee lists, registration records, and sign-in records;
- LPSC correspondence with event organizers;
- records identifying participating utilities, electric cooperatives, nuclear companies/developers/vendors, consultants, trade associations, legislators, and state agencies;
- LPSC travel, lodging, meal, registration, sponsorship, reimbursement, or related expenses;
- records identifying who paid for or sponsored event-related expenses.

The request references the LPSC's May 22, 2024 minutes, which state that the June Business & Executive session would be scheduled in Metairie to coincide with the Louisiana Advanced Nuclear Competitive Edge project/event on June 17–18.

Electronic production was requested. If material is withheld, reasonably segregable non-exempt portions and the statutory basis for withholding were requested. A fee estimate was requested before costs exceed $25.
