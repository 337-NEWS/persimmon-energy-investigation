# Methodology

## Purpose

This archive allows readers to inspect the documentary basis of 337.NEWS reporting arising from the Persimmon Energy Center investigation.

## Verification

Primary documentation is preferred. Statements about what a person said should, where practicable, be supported by the original post, video, transcript, meeting record, filing, or contemporaneous screenshot.

A source's presence here establishes relevance to the reporting, not wrongdoing.

## Chronology and inference

Dates should be recorded precisely when supported. Temporal proximity is not treated as evidence of causation, coordination, or quid pro quo.

Campaign contributions, lobbying expenditures, meetings, facility tours, procurement activity, and public statements should be reported as separate facts unless documentary evidence supports a stronger connection.

## Screenshots and social media

Screenshots may be preserved as primary evidence when they document public statements. Record the account name, visible date or relative date, retrieval date, URL when available, and enough surrounding context to avoid misleading quotation.

## Document handling

Preserve original files where legally and ethically appropriate. Record source URL and retrieval date where practicable. Important files may be accompanied by SHA-256 hashes.

Do not substantively alter evidence. Disclose material redactions, conversions, or processing.

## Privacy

Review records before public upload for sensitive identifiers, personal contact information, signatures, account information, or unrelated private information whose republication is unnecessary to the public-interest purpose.

## Working hypotheses

Working theories belong in `reporter-notes/` and must be labeled **REPORTER NOTES**. They are not findings and should not be presented as established facts without supporting evidence.

## Corrections

Substantive errors should be corrected transparently in `corrections.md`. Git history should preserve the prior version and correction.

## Right of response

Before publication of material allegations or significant conclusions about identifiable people or organizations, 337.NEWS should make reasonable efforts to obtain comment and accurately represent material responses.
