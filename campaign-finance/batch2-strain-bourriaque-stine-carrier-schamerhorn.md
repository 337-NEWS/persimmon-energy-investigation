# Batch 2 — Energy-related campaign contributions

**Source:** Louisiana Ethics Administration Campaign Finance E-Filer  
**Research date:** October 5, 2026  
**Classification:** REPORTER RESEARCH / PUBLIC CAMPAIGN-FINANCE RECORDS

## Method and limitations

Each filer's e-filed contribution records were reviewed using the most-recent contribution page plus targeted contributor-name searches including Entergy, ENPAC, Solar, LNG, Nuclear, Exxon, Chevron, Shell, Cleco, SWEPCO, Oil, Gas, Energy, Pipeline, Electric, Utility, Chemical, Petro, Cooperative, Midstream, LACRE, ALEC, Atmos, Cheniere and related terms.

Pagination beyond page 1 was intermittently unreliable in the Ethics portal, so older entries were located using contributor-name keyword searches rather than guaranteed exhaustive page-by-page review. Energy-sector contributions from people or entities whose names do not reveal their industry may therefore be absent.

Unless otherwise noted below, listed hits were recorded as monetary contributions. Headline entries should be spot-verified against the underlying filed campaign reports before publication.

**Interpretive caution:** A lawful campaign contribution does not establish influence, coordination, quid pro quo, or agreement with the contributor's policy preferences. This file exists to permit consistent comparison across officials.
