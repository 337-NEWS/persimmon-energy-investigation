## Official purpose

HR286 urges and requests Louisiana Economic Development to study the feasibility of a **Louisiana Rural Community Energy and Education Investment Program**.

The original resolution directs LED to examine a program involving energy systems, energy-efficiency improvements, building modernization, workforce-development infrastructure, financing mechanisms, rural prioritization, performance standards, and public reporting.

The official House digest describes the contemplated program as involving tax incentives and public-private partnerships to finance renewable energy, energy efficiency, and workforce-infrastructure improvements in rural communities.

## Areas the original resolution asks LED to study

1. Tax-credit structures and other financing mechanisms intended to attract private capital with minimal or no up-front taxpayer cost.
2. Eligible investments in energy systems, efficiency, building modernization, and workforce infrastructure suited to rural Louisiana.
3. Prioritization for economically distressed parishes and public institutions.
4. Performance standards, projected public-entity savings, and economic-impact analysis.
5. Coordination with existing federal programs.
6. Measurable performance indicators, savings verification, and annual public reporting.

The resolution also calls for input from the Department of Revenue, Louisiana Works, the Public Service Commission, and other appropriate agencies, with a written report contemplated by February 1, 2027.

## Investigative relevance

This resolution is important because it complicates any simplistic characterization of Owen as categorically “anti-solar.” His September 2026 public argument criticizes industrial-scale rural solar siting and reliance on intermittent generation, while HR286 explicitly contemplates study of renewable-energy and efficiency investments in rural communities.

That suggests a more precise policy distinction worth tracking: **scale, siting, land use, reliability, financing, and community benefit**, rather than renewable energy versus non-renewable energy as an absolute binary.

No inference of coordination with any developer, utility, cooperative, or other person should be drawn from the resolution alone.
