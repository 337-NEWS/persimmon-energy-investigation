## Official purpose

HR144 requests that the Louisiana Board of Regents provide for a **rigorous, peer-reviewed study** concerning the potential effects of mixing carbon streams captured from industrial sites and from the air on water supplies and the ecological environment, with reports to the House Committee on Natural Resources and Environment.

## Investigative relevance

HR144 places Owen directly in Louisiana's 2026 debate over carbon capture and environmental risk. The resolution is relevant to the wider energy-policy investigation because Southwest Louisiana is simultaneously confronting utility-scale solar development, carbon-capture/storage proposals, large new electric loads, and renewed discussion of nuclear generation.

The resolution should be treated as evidence of Owen's stated preference for additional scientific review of a carbon-capture issue—not as evidence that he opposes carbon capture categorically.
