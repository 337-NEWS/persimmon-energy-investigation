# Persimmon Energy Investigation

This repository is the research and documentary archive for an ongoing **337.NEWS** investigation originating with the proposed Persimmon Energy Center in northern Calcasieu Parish, Louisiana.

The reporting has expanded into related questions involving utility-scale solar, land use, electric-system capacity, data-center load, nuclear generation, electric cooperatives, Louisiana energy policy, lobbying, campaign finance, and public messaging.

This repository exists for **source transparency and accountability**. It is an evidence archive, not a statement that any person or organization has engaged in wrongdoing.

## Investigative questions

Among the questions being examined:

- How is the Persimmon Energy Center being presented to the public, regulators, landowners, and elected officials?
- How have Southwest Louisiana officials approached utility-scale solar and large data-center developments?
- Why are nuclear generation and advanced nuclear technologies increasingly appearing in the same policy conversation?
- What roles do Entergy, 1803 Electric Cooperative, electric cooperatives, developers, regulators, legislators, trade groups, and other organizations play in that discussion?
- What do public records, campaign-finance filings, lobbying disclosures, meeting records, procurement records, and contemporaneous statements establish about the chronology?

These are investigative questions, **not predetermined conclusions**.

## Evidence standards

- **PRIMARY SOURCE** — original government record, filing, meeting record, campaign-finance report, correspondence, testimony, corporate document, screenshot, video, database record, or other original evidence.
- **SECONDARY SOURCE** — reporting or analysis produced by another publication or organization.
- **REPORTER NOTES** — working questions, calculations, hypotheses, leads, or analysis. Reporter notes are not findings of fact.

Where possible, important claims should trace back to primary documentation.

## Important context

Inclusion of a person, company, contribution, payment, statement, meeting, or document in this repository **does not imply misconduct or coordination**.

Temporal proximity is not proof of causation. Campaign contributions do not by themselves establish influence. Attendance at a meeting or facility tour does not by itself establish agreement with the host. Public statements are preserved in context whenever practicable.

Documents may be incomplete, superseded, corrected, or supplemented as reporting continues.
